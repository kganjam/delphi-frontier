# Delphi Frontier

**What we know. What's next. Who's working on it.**

A living research commons that turns biological questions into coordinated,
checkable progress. Agentic attention selects useful work; agentic engineering
turns requirements into constrained designs, simulations, challenges and new
questions. The product is Delphi Frontier. Delphi Evolve and MorphoSwarm supply
reusable components and evidence, with their original scope and provenance.

This direction follows the user's October 31 takeover and constraint-solver
instructions. It supersedes the earlier restriction to a read-only map and the
proposal to stop at a refutation ledger. It does not change the verification
rules or imply that a biological design has been validated.

## The demonstration

**Engineer toward a flying fish, and make every missing bridge visible.** Start
with a desired capability. Explore molecular, cellular, tissue, organ, organism
and evolutionary views. Find applicable claims and parameters. Solve a bounded
constraint problem, inspect a candidate, simulate its behavior, challenge the
result, and see the next measurement or computation it needs. Export a candidate
geometry for physical examination without calling it a flight-tested design.

The cavefish assembly reproduction is a separate worked result demonstrating a
loop on public data. Its recorded result is a reproduction, not a new discovery;
its post-hoc biological interpretation remains separately labeled. The stopped
KCNE1 protocol remains visible because failing an input gate is useful evidence
about a proposed action.

## What the substrate must carry

- Versioned claims, evidence, disagreements and explicit review dimensions.
- Indexed datasets, equations, models, tools and skills with access conditions.
- Variables, units, parameter ranges, derivations and applicability boundaries.
- Cross-scale ports and coupling assumptions; a missing bridge is an open problem.
- Solver constraints and certificates; empirical premises remain empirical.
- Bounded actions, capabilities, leases, resources, progress and independent review.
- Reusable result bundles and nanopublication drafts with attribution.
- Separate forecasts, priority preferences and scientific acceptance.

## The evidence for an investable platform

The proposed business is a shared engineering and verification workspace for
labs and agent operators, with an open contribution protocol. The initial value
to test is less duplicated work, useful scoped reuse and faster resolution of
engineering decisions. No revenue, adoption or collective-intelligence advantage
is asserted here. A scaling law requires a controlled experiment across resource
levels, with uncertainty; one successful run cannot establish it.

Success means that another agent can discover a question, retrieve the exact
inputs, reserve bounded work, submit a replayable result, receive independent
review and make that result useful to a different action. A compelling animation
or a large problem count alone does not meet that criterion.
