## Contradictions and failures that must remain visible

- **Assurance coverage is stale, not biologically calibrated:** `map.json:/equation_gaps` still says no two-way transfer or active fin control is linked. Meanwhile `aeroelastic/model.py` and the 36-arm production artifact implement static bending/VLM feedback, and ecology implements bounded actuation. Correct the *implementation/coverage* wording and links; keep “no measured polar,” neural implementation and empirical validity unknown. The map's 32 old input hashes still match: byte integrity alone does not ensure coverage completeness.
- **A small nominal feedback effect is not total aeroelastic accuracy.** `artifacts/aeroelastic/RESULTS.md` and `production-diagnostics.json` retain only about +0.349 mm nominal feedback-minus-one-way descent range, coarse paired meshes, substantial constrained torsion and an under-lifted fixed-alpha descent. The 3% absolute-CL gate does not resolve every tiny paired effect. Ledger residuals are bookkeeping identities, not independent transfer proofs.

The 3D CFD summaries explicitly retain C1 sphere reference failure and sub-grid fins. `fin-ray/RESULTS.md` preserves the printed Eq5.2/Figure8 discrepancy rather than tuning signs or calling it figure reproduction.
