## What is actually proved

The definitions are `C0 − a R²/(6D)` and `C0 − a L²/(2D)`. Two parametric equivalences
show their centers are nonnegative iff `a R² ≤ 6 D C0` and `a L² ≤ 2 D C0`, respectively,
under the stated positive diffusion and domain premises. Four further statements cover
zero-size boundary values and equality at the limits: **six statements, two geometry laws**.
The two corrupted coefficients (6→5 and 2→1) give **four rejected controls**: two failed
parametric proof steps and two explicit rational counterexample witnesses. These counts
must not be described as six independent biological results or four distinct mutant laws.

At equality the center is **zero**, not above a physiological viability threshold. Negative
continuations are out-of-domain constant-consumption diagnostics, not physical negative
oxygen. The real-valued Lean functions are not unit-typed: dimensional interpretation is
an external contract, not something these theorems prove.

The contract's mass-basis units are coherent: `D` is m²/s, `C0` m³ O2/kg tissue, and `a`
m³ O2/(kg tissue·s), so `a R²` and `D C0` match and `C0/a` is time. Any per-volume
interpretation must convert both consistently. The slab length is the center-to-supplied-face
distance (half-thickness for a symmetric two-sided slab), not automatically total thickness.

The source interpretation is explicit: Grimes et al. (2014), Eq. 2.6, supplies the spherical
limit after `C0 = p0/Omega`; the slab is a separately derived constant-consumption analogue.
The DLD1 tumor-spheroid fitted parameters are not transferred. Lean proves neither the PDE
solution nor the article-to-equation translation, boundary-condition applicability, steady
state, homogeneous consumption, diffusion coefficient, supply, or tissue viability.
