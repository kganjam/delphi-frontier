# Print law index: what each downloadable print file is grounded in, and what was checked

Generated by `python -m engineering.print_law_index` from `artifacts/print-law-index.json` (schema `frontier.print-law-index/1`, `artifacts/print-law-index.schema.json`). Do not edit by hand.

**Read this first.** A law listed for a file says how the file relates to it (governing, checked, inspired_only, not_applicable); it is not evidence that the printed shape satisfies the law. Masses are calculated from mesh volume times a card density and were not weighed. No part has been printed, weighed, glided or thrown; no launch is cleared; the lift, drag, stability, beam and catch numbers are hand models or simulated scenarios with assumed coefficients (C_L in particular depends on geometry and flow and generally comes from experiment). Failed thresholds are reported as failed. Frozen revisions are read, never edited; inconsistencies in their prose are listed under findings, not corrected.

## How the mass is calculated
- **Per part:** mesh volume (divergence theorem on the closed STL) × the cured density. Glider parts use Alpha Grey V1 at 1220 kg/m³, from root's material card; the card's 1110 kg/m³ liquid density is not used.
- **Per assembly:** the parts are moved into the fish frame by their recorded transforms and summed. The CG is the mass-weighted mean.
- **Ballast:** the dry assembly plus nose ballast from the frozen manifest's 52 mm row, at an assumed 4.7 g/cm³.
- **Display props:** the resin is not chosen, so their mass is unknown. A conditional mass, assuming a fully dense print at 1220 kg/m³, is given separately.
- **Nothing was weighed.** Every figure here is calculated from a mesh.

## Why every glider revision fails its mass budget
There are two budgets. Each comes from the lift equation at 8 m/s with an assumed C_L of 0.5:
- the frozen **legacy 10.5 g**, derived on v3a's 52.9 cm² of paired fin area, and kept as recorded;
- the **area-specific cap**, recomputed here on each revision's own paired area.

- **Where the mass is.** The hollow body is 10.95–13.76 g. The four paired fins are 6.19–7.38 g together.
- **Why there is ballast.** The dry CG sits behind the hand neutral-point bracket, so the fish is statically unstable dry under that bracket. Nose ballast moves the CG forward, and it adds 5.7–8.9 g.
- **What the budget means.** It is a hand trim budget, not a flight result. Passing it would not show that the fish glides; failing it says the fish would need a faster launch or a higher C_L than assumed.

## Findings (kept visible, not resolved)
- **right_hand_print_files_are_left_parts** (recorded defect): Every _R print STL of r1-r3 holds the left part (det -1 export pose). Kept byte for byte; fixed in r4-daane-printfix. Fish-frame mass properties, GLBs and flight models were not affected.
- **body_thin_wall_near_forward_catch_slot** (unresolved): Reviewer claude-13: readings down to 0.097 mm (0.115 mm twice on r3) at the x 20 slot's lateral openings, judged feather edges rather than a thin wall; they can print rough, chip, or cut the band. The builder's sampler classed its own 0.31 mm reading there as edge-limited and did not gate it. r1's body has a reviewer reading of 0.317 mm aft of its catch slot, also edge-limited.
- **biological_constraints_kept_names_v3a** (frozen prose inconsistency): biological_constraints.kept reads 'v3a outline, ray count, ray roots, angles and lengths, fin positions and dihedral', but source_candidate is fish3d-v3c-daane-length. The outline_check itself compared against the v3c candidate's own rays.json (builder code), so the check result refers to v3c; the prose string is stale. The frozen manifests are not edited; a later revision will derive the string from the candidate.
- **assembly_md_cg_window_superseded** (superseded guidance): The frozen ASSEMBLY.md cites a 44-60 mm CG window and lists a 60 mm ballast row. That window came from v3a's planform. For r3/r4 claude-07's constraint is CG x 49.5-55.2 mm, target 52.4, never aft of 54.1 mm before the VLM NP, so the 56 and 60 mm rows are outside it.
- **legacy_mass_threshold** (superseded guidance): The frozen 10.5 g threshold was derived on v3a's 52.9 cm2 paired area. It is retained as a legacy threshold; each assembly also carries its own area-specific cap, recomputed here. Every revision fails both.
