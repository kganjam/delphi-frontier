re-read rather than re-remembered.

`full-text` · `partial-text` (specific sections read in the archived PDF) · `abstract` · `listing` ·

## 1. The LAMM stack (Buehler group, MIT) — the systems we build on

These four are the direct prior art for this project's architecture. The group wrote ScienceClaw and
Infinite, and the hackathon brief points at them.

### [B1] Wang, Marom, Pal, Luu, Lu, Berkovich, Buehler (2026)
**Autonomous Agents Coordinating Distributed Discovery Through Emergent Artifact Exchange**
arXiv:2603.14312v1, 15 Mar 2026. Laboratory for Atomistic and Molecular Mechanics (LAMM), MIT.
→ `pdf/arxiv-2603.14312.pdf` · **read-level: partial-text** (title page and author list read here;
the body is summarised in `morphoswarm:docs/prior-art/scienceclaw-infinite.md`, which reports the
paper gives **qualitative outcomes only — no quantitative metrics or benchmark numbers**)

The ScienceClaw + Infinite paper. Four autonomous investigations: SSTR2 peptide design,
impact-resistant ceramics, a biology/materials/music "resonance" bridge, and an urban-morphology ↔
grain-boundary analogy. **Lee Marom** is the second author (LAMM / Dept. of Mechanical Engineering).

### [B2] Wang & Buehler (2026)
**Self-Revising Discovery Systems for Science: A Categorical Framework for Agentic Artificial Intelligence**
arXiv:2606.01444v2, 21 Aug 2026.
→ `pdf/arxiv-2606.01444.pdf` · **read-level: partial-text** (abstract and §1 Introduction read in
the archived PDF; the Builder/Breaker and CategoryScienceClaw case studies are **not yet read**)

The category-theory paper, and the most important single reference for our design. Its claims, as
the authors state them:

- Scientific state in a fixed regime is a **copresheaf** `I_t : Ŝ → Set` over a schema category `Ŝ`;
  realised provenance is the **category of elements** `∫_Ŝ I_t`, or a multicategorical analogue for
  multi-parent artifacts.
- **Search** updates that state *within the same schema*.
- **Discovery** is a verified transition `u : Ŝ → Ŝ′` that preserves accepted prior artifacts,
  transports old evidence by **left Kan extension**, and **exposes residual content not generated by
  transport**.
- Therefore discovery is "a change in admissible representation, rather than a subjective novelty
  judgment or a higher model score."
- Instantiated as **Builder/Breaker** (revises a protein-mechanics symbolic world model under a
  minimum-description-length gate, *retaining rejected edits and retractions*) and
  **CategoryScienceClaw** (ScienceClaw plus proof-carrying typed artifacts, lineage, gates, stress
  tests, claim records).

*Our inference, not theirs:* this is a direct critique of the evaluation kernel in
[the design loop](../architecture/the-design-loop.md). A frozen rubric ranks within a fixed
hypothesis space, which is exactly the operation the authors identify as *search*. See
[categorical-regime-transitions.md](../architecture/categorical-regime-transitions.md).

### [B3] Buehler (2025)
**Agentic Deep Graph Reasoning Yields Self-Organizing Knowledge Networks**
arXiv:2502.13025v1, 18 Feb 2025.
→ `pdf/arxiv-2502.13025.pdf` · **read-level: partial-text** (title page and abstract read here)

Agentic autonomous graph expansion: a reasoning model coupled to a continually updated graph,
generating concepts and relations and merging them into a global graph. Reports emergent hub and
bridge concepts.

### [B4] Buehler (2025)
**In-situ Graph Reasoning and Knowledge Expansion Using Graph-PReFLexOR**
arXiv:2501.08120v1, 14 Jan 2025.
→ `pdf/arxiv-2501.08120.pdf` · **read-level: partial-text** (title page and abstract read here)

Graph reasoning plus symbolic abstraction, explicitly category-theory-inspired: concepts as nodes,
relationships as edges, supporting hierarchical inference. Demonstrated on materials design and
hypothesis generation.

## 2. Sources held elsewhere in this organisation

### [B5] `morphoswarm:docs/prior-art/scienceclaw-infinite.md`
**read-level: full-text** (read 2026-11-03)

A verified reading of both codebases, with line-level citations. The load-bearing item for us is
ScienceClaw's **pressure score**, quoted verbatim from `artifacts/pressure.py:194`:

```python
return float(2.0 * novelty + 1.0 * centrality + 0.5 * float(depth) + 0.2 * age_term)
```

where `novelty = 1/(1+coverage)`, `centrality` is the count of similar open needs capped at 6.0,
`depth` is parent depth in the artifact DAG, and `age_term = log1p(age_minutes)` — the last of which
exists to prevent starvation. **No LLM is in this loop**, which is what makes the coordination
reproducible and auditable.

Also records: the `Artifact` dataclass (`artifacts/artifact.py:509-531`) with `content_hash` =
sha256 over canonical JSON, `parent_artifact_ids` for DAG lineage, and `needs`; append-only JSONL
storage; a 336-entry skill registry in which **none covers morphogenesis**; and a licence
inconsistency in Infinite (the `LICENSE` file is Apache-2.0, the README's closing section says MIT).

### [B6] Weidener et al. — the Moltbook agent-population study
**read-level: could-not-access** (not retrieved; the figure below is quoted from

## 4. Conventions

1. **Archive the PDF.** A URL is not a source; it rots, paywalls, or serves a CAPTCHA. Cloud agents
   on this project measured `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` returning **HTTP 200 with a reCAPTCHA page
   instead of the article** — a status code alone would have recorded that as a successful read.
2. **Record the read-level in the same line as the claim**, not in a footnote.
3. **Separate their claim from our inference** in the sentence where the inference is drawn.
4. **A negative or unresolved citation stays visible** (§3) rather than being dropped.
