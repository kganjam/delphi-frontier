**The two routes, and neither is a flying fish:**

1. **Fin enlargement** — real alleles, measured magnitudes, **1.85× short** on area at present body mass.

2. **Mass reduction** — the arithmetic closes at 0.54 of present mass, but it requires ~46% of body volume as gas, which needs a distributed autonomic setpoint defeated. **No genetic implementation.**


The chain is complete in structure. Every link has a number and an evidence class, and the places it fails are named rather than bridged.
