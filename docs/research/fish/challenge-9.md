# Challenge 9 — adversarial review of the Boltz2 kcnk5b confidence claim

**Claim under review (not mine):** "Boltz2 with an MMseqs2/PDB70 MSA predicts the kcnk5b fold at
mean pLDDT 62.0, pTM 0.479; the confident block is residues ~1-240 and everything past ~260 is
poor."
Recorded evidence level: `simulated`. Recorded value: `0.479 pTM`.
Recorded source: Nebius workbench `boltz2_predict_native` + `msa_search_native`.

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** The two headline numbers are real and I reproduced one of them to three
decimal places from the committed structure. What fails is everything the claim is *for*: the
regional reading ("confident block ~1-240 / poor past ~260") is the exact statement the source
branch already **retracted in writing** as an MSA-coverage artifact, the "confident block" is not
a block, and Boltz2 is the weakest of three predictions of this same sequence that the repository
holds.

Everything below separates **the author's claim**, **the source record**, and **my own
recomputation**. All structural numbers I report were recomputed by me from CA-atom
`B_iso_or_equiv` fields; I did not read the author's working before computing.

---

## 0. Locating the artifact (and a false start worth recording)

My first sweep found `results/mol-sims/claude-20261101/structures/kcnk5b_dimer_boltz2_failed/`
on `origin/claude/mol-sims-20261101`, where Boltz-2 **failed** on kcnk5b
(`NATIVE_UPSTREAM_FAILED` / `ValueError: All proteins with the same sequence must share the same
MSA!`). I was one step from writing "Boltz2 never produced a kcnk5b structure; the claim
misattributes OpenFold3 numbers." **That would have been a manufactured fault.** It is a
different experiment: a 2×(Q6PFU3 1–265) homodimer, retried with OpenFold3 (chain pLDDT 69.8,
pTM 0.556, ipTM 0.476).

The claim's actual source is `origin/swarm-architecture`:
`docs/research/fish/proteins-results.md` and `results/fish-proteins/structures/kcnk5b_boltz2.cif`
(monomer, full 448 residues). I record the near-miss because "0.479" sits one digit from that
dimer's ipTM 0.47568, and a reviewer in a hurry would have called a coincidence a forgery.

**Scope of the negative search:** `git grep` for `kcnk5b` and for `boltz2.*kcnk5b` across all
**191** remote refs of `kganjam/delphi-frontier`. Boltz2-kcnk5b artifacts exist on exactly two
branches: the failed dimer receipt (`claude/mol-sims-20261101`) and the monomer CIF + report
table (`swarm-architecture`). No other copy exists in the repository.

## 2. The proxy: pLDDT is standing in for MSA coverage, not for order

This is the claim's load-bearing fault, and **the source branch states it before I do.**
`proteins-results.md` carries a section headed *"CORRECTION: the 'disordered C-terminal tail' was
an MSA-coverage artifact"*:

| region | mean MSA depth (source) | Boltz2 pLDDT | ESMFold2 pLDDT |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–250 | 21.4 | 77.5 | 89.2 |
| 261–448 | **0.5** | 41.4 | **76.4** |

with "MSA depth is exactly 0.0 from residue 326 onward", and the author's own conclusion:
"Boltz2's low confidence tracks *where its alignment ran out*, not where the protein is
disordered… **the Boltz2 signal was never evidence about disorder.**"

The claim under review reproduces the pre-correction reading. Its clause "everything past ~260 is
poor" is **numerically true of Boltz2's output** (my recomputation: 261–448 mean 41.39, 0 % of
188 residues ≥70) and **void as a statement about kcnk5b**, because past residue 326 the model
had nothing to align to. The right quantity is per-position alignment depth, and the source
measured it. A reader who meets the claim without the correction attached will draw the inference
the author has already withdrawn.

Secondary proxy, same direction: the PDB70 hit set (`kcnk5b-HITS.md`, 71 hits / 72 A3M rows)
contains **6WLV and 6WM0 — TASK2 (gene `Kcnk5`), the direct ortholog**, at 64.3 % identity over
99.2 % of the query in the sibling branch's measurement. So the confident region is where a
near-ortholog crystal structure aligns. The companion branch draws the general lesson explicitly
("a PDB70-only MSA invites template memorisation"). The claim's confident/poor split is closer to
a **templated/untemplated** split than to a folded/unfolded one.

## 3. The hidden assumption: that "the confident block is residues ~1-240" describes a block

Relaxed by looking inside the range instead of averaging over it. My per-20-residue profile of
Boltz2 pLDDT:

```
  1- 20: 84 (85% ≥70)    81-100: 84 (90%)    161-180: 87 (100%)
 21- 40: 81 (90%)       101-120: 81 (90%)    181-200: 91 (100%)
 41- 60: 66 (25%)       121-140: 80 (80%)    201-220: 81 ( 90%)
 61- 80: 49 ( 0%)       141-160: 64 (10%)    221-240: 87 (100%)
                                             241-260: 64 ( 10%)
```

Residues 1–240 average 78.0, but **68 of those 240 residues (28.3 %) are below 70**, and the
range contains five separate ≥70 runs, not one: 4–33, 83–101, 104–136, 160–214, 217–240.
Decisively, **residues 61–80 average 49 with 0 % at ≥70** — the same band as the C-terminal
region the claim calls "poor" (41.4). The claim's dichotomy therefore fails on its own terms: a
20-residue stretch *inside* the "confident block" is as poor as the region outside it. The
largest genuinely contiguous confident unit is **55 aa at 160–214**, which is 12 % of the
protein, not 54 %.

I could not test whether the 61–80 and 141–160 troughs are *also* coverage troughs: the A3M is
not committed and the source's depth table is reported only as region means
(read-level: could-not-access). That is the obvious next measurement.

## 4. Is the evidence level honest?

`simulated` is the correct kind, and the source document is unusually scrupulous — its own
header reads "pLDDT and pTM are each model's confidence in its own output. They are not accuracy
and not measurements." Two defects survive that:

1. **"predicts the kcnk5b fold" is not a simulated-level verb.** pLDDT/pTM are model
   self-assessment. Nothing here was compared against an experimental kcnk5b structure (the
   source says so). The honest form is "Boltz2 reports mean pLDDT 62.0 on its own prediction",
   not "predicts the fold at 62.0".
2. **The recorded value `0.479 pTM` has no committed receipt** (§1). A derived or
   unretrievable number occupying the ledger's value field, beside a fully checkable 62.0, is a
   defect of the kind this review exists to catch, even though I have no reason to think 0.479 is
   wrong.

## 5. Regime and counter-examples the claim misses

**Counter-example inside the same branch, on the identical 448-residue sequence.** ESMFold2 —
a protein language model, **no MSA at all** — beats Boltz2 on every internal metric. My
recomputation from `kcnk5b_esmfold2.cif`:

| | Boltz2 + PDB70 | **ESMFold2** |
|---|---|---|
| mean pLDDT | 62.03 | **83.63** |
| pTM | 0.479 (unverified) | **0.6067** (receipt committed) |
| residues ≥70 | 38.8 % | **90.0 %** (89.96 % strict `>70`) |
| residues <50 | 33.0 % | **0.9 %** |
| longest run ≥70 | 55 aa (160–214) | **87 aa (157–243)** |
| mean 261–448 | 41.4 | **76.4** |
| TIGYG / TVGFG | 80.2 / 82.6 | **96.5 / 96.3** |

Presenting Boltz2 as the prediction of "the kcnk5b fold" selects the **worst of three available
models** without saying so. The claim is not false about Boltz2; it is unrepresentative of what
the repository knows about this protein.

**Regime: in this lane Boltz2's pLDDT is largely a function of MSA depth.** Same runtime, same
branch, same report table: and1 (570 aa, depth 1) → 38.1 and 0.0 % ≥70; and2 (508 aa, depth 1) →
36.1 and 0.0 % ≥70, both *below* the single-sequence OpenFold2 lane (47.6, 44.3). kcnk5b, with
depth 72, lands at 62.0. So "62.0 with a PDB70 MSA" is better read as a coverage statistic than
as a fold assessment — and the claim's phrase "with an MMseqs2/PDB70 MSA" hides the two numbers
that drive the result: depth **72**, falling to **0 from residue 326**.

**Taxon/assembly regime the claim does not mention.** A K2P channel conducts as a dimer. This is
a monomer prediction, so its pTM says nothing about the physiological assembly — and the
repository's one attempt at that assembly (OpenFold3, 2×1–265) returned ipTM **0.476**, below the
0.6 line the same branch set for usability, and no MD or variant work was run on it. "The kcnk5b
fold" is doing quiet work for "a monomer backbone of an unreviewed 448-aa TrEMBL record".

**A small point in the claim's favour, stated because it is true:** both *alf* missense residues
sit in confident territory in both models — Boltz2 pLDDT 82.7 at W169 and 76.2 at F241 (ESMFold2:
85.5 and 86.5). The claim's boundary "~1-240" would have put F241 just outside the confident
block; the per-residue value shows it is not. (Allele identities W169L / F241Y are from a sibling
branch's record of Perathoner et al. 2014, *PLoS Genet* 10:e1004080, PMID 24453984 — **full-text**
per that branch's read-level annotation; I did not re-retrieve the paper.)

## 6. Disagreement preserved

- **Boltz2 vs ESMFold2 on residues 261–448**: 41.4 vs 76.4. These do not average to anything. One
  model had no alignment there; the other needs none. Neither is a measurement of order.
- **The source branch vs the claim**: the source says the C-terminal signal "was never evidence
  about disorder"; the claim still reports "everything past ~260 is poor" as a finding. I have not
  reconciled these, because the correction postdates and supersedes the reading.
- **ESMFold2's 76.4 is not a positive finding either** — the source is explicit that it "does not
  prove the tail is ordered in vivo". I leave that open.

---

## 7. What would settle it

1. Commit the Boltz2 confidence receipt, closing the pTM 0.479 provenance gap.
2. Commit the A3M and publish per-position alignment depth, which tests whether the 61–80 and
   141–160 troughs inside the "confident block" are coverage troughs too. If they are, the single
   mechanism explains the whole profile and pLDDT should not be quoted regionally at all here.
3. Run Boltz2 on 1–325 (the covered region) alone. If the 1–240 numbers hold, the C-terminal
   collapse is confined to the uncovered tail; if they rise, even the "confident block" was being
   dragged by the untemplated remainder.
4. Hold out 6WLV/6WM0 from the MSA and re-run. That separates template recall from inference, and
   it is the measurement the repository's own template-recall finding on ShhN implies is needed.
