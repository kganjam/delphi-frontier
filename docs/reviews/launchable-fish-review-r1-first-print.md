## Blocking before anything is printed
1. **The right-hand fins are copies of the left-hand fins, not mirrors.** `pectoral_R.stl` has exactly the vertex set and triangle order of `pectoral_L.stl`, and likewise every `pelvic_R-dec*.stl` against `pelvic_L-dec*.stl`. The manifest then places each R part with a `print_to_fish_transform` whose determinant is -1 (the L transform with the y row negated). In the fish frame the R meshes are the exact y-mirror of the L meshes (checked, 0.0 mm deviation), so the GLB and mass properties are right, but a printed object cannot be reflected: two prints from these files are two left-hand fins. The fins are chiral (spar under the membrane, swept leading edge, keyed root tongue, 2 degree incidence), and no rotation turns one into the other. Required: write mirrored R STLs (reflect and reverse winding) so each print-pose part is a proper-rotation placement, or state that the slicer must mirror them. I verified the geometry facts; whether PreForm offers a mirror operation I did not check.
2. **The catch has no load calculation.** The manifest gives geometry only (slot 3.5 mm wide raked 30 degrees, seat at x = 28 mm, a 2 mm liner wall, contact radius 1.75 mm); the 3bd7f24 hook sizing is for a stem, not this slot, and nothing sizes the liner. Peak catch force on the ballasted 28.9 g assembly is 28.9 N in the module's worst stack (10 m/s, 0.1 m stroke, peak factor 2), 6.2 N at 8 m/s over 0.3 m with factor 2, 0.8 N at 4 m/s over 0.3 m with factor 1. The liner's height is not in the manifest, so I cannot give a stress; I state the load and the missing number.
3. **The seat offset is small and sets the launch pitch rate.** The seat is 1.509 mm below the dry CG, which is the number the manifest quotes, but the launch happens with ballast: the ballasted CG (52.0, z 2.744 mm; ballast 8.93 g at the bay centroid, Iyy 4.87e-5 kg m^2 by my parallel-axis arithmetic) sits 0.935 mm above the seat. If the band line stays that far from the CG, the pitch rate at release is about 2.2, 3.3 and 4.4 rad/s (127, 191, 254 degrees per second) at 4, 6 and 8 m/s, i.e. 2.4–4.7 rad/s per millimetre of offset. A 1 mm error in the measured CG height therefore changes the release pitch rate by about as much as the whole nominal value. This is an arithmetic bound under a constant-offset assumption, not a dynamics result, and the dynamics are 07's.

## Findings that do not block a flagged publication
4. **The prototype is far over its own mass limit.** The manifest's `within_mass_limit` is false and says so: limit 10.5 g (07's hand number); strong dry 19.95 g, ballasted 28.88 g; light dry 17.14 g, ballasted 24.03 g. The hand trim speeds are 13.2 m/s (strong, ballasted, CL 0.5) and 12.1 m/s (light), outside the 4–8 m/s scenarios, and 11.0 / 10.2 m/s even dry. Publishing with "over mass limit, not trim-able in the declared scenarios" is accurate; "launchable" is not yet.
5. **Ballast numbers are assumptions.** The bay (3030 mm^3) holds at most about 14.2 g at the assumed 4.7 g/cm^3 (steel shot in glue, ~60% packing, unmeasured); CG targets of 44–47 mm are infeasible in the manifest's own table; the reachable CG is 48.0 to 69.3 mm. The CG window the module asks for (44–60 mm) is therefore not fully reachable.
6. **A 0.317 mm wall reading sits at the catch.** The body's sampled minimum, 0.317 mm (below the 0.45 mm rule), is at (29.48, 9.61, -4.53) mm, 1.5 mm aft of the slot (x = 28). The manifest classes it "edge-limited" and passes `min_wall_ok`; it is in the launch load path. The wall sampler covers sampled faces only (3000 samples).
7. **Root strength rests on a fixed-root assumption.** The fins attach by a keyed tongue (pectoral 2 mm thick, 10 mm long, 6 mm engagement, 0.1 mm clearance, glue advised). On the 3bd7f24 worst-case root moments (0.0195 N m pectoral, 0.0031 N m pelvic), the tongue section alone sees about 2.9 and 0.8 MPa (allowable 14.3 MPa under the declared factor of 3), so the tongue is not the weak link on that arithmetic; but the joint stiffness, the glue shear, the 0.8 mm socket shell and the 0.1 mm printer tolerance are untested, and 3bd7f24 treats the root as clamped. The pelvic root chord is 2.9 mm while the tongue is 6 mm long, which the manifest does not reconcile (a root fairing block is mentioned).
8. **Ridge fillets are chamfers, not radii** (the manifest says so); the stress-concentration allowance is the same unvalidated factor of 3.
9. **Frame statement.** The manifest now states a right-handed frame in which +y is the anatomical right and v3's `_L` names the anatomical right. That agrees with my earlier handedness finding; the R/L mirror problem in item 1 is the printed-part consequence.

## Verdict structure
Numerical dimensions (volume, mass, CG, inertia, units, closure) pass on my independent recomputation for both revisions. A flagged publication of the files as a provisional, untested, over-mass prototype is consistent with what the manifests say, provided item 1 is fixed or stated, since two prints would not make a pair. Replication, empirical relevance and flight survival stay insufficient; nothing is marked not applicable.
