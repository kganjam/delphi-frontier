## What is checked and what is not

- Smooth file: gated on the validator, the 3,000- and 12,000-sample wall readings, extremities and mirror symmetry.
- Scale-relief file: gated on the validator and extremities. Its 0.2–0.4 mm scale relief is texture below the wall rule by design, so its wall readings and symmetry are reported, not gated.
- Stand: gated on the validator, both wall samples and extremities.
- Print preparation is unvalidated: printer and resin are unknown; nothing has been sliced, loaded into PreForm or printed.
- Wall rules are provisional, configurable prop choices (1.0 mm; 1.5 mm for the reinforced prop), checked on sampled faces only (3,000 plus 12,000 samples, or every face of a mesh with fewer faces); a thinner region between samples can be missed.
- Membranes and rays are thickened for printing; they are not biological thickness. Flow and fin-ray work use the 1:1 candidate files, not these props.
- The -scales file adds 0.2-0.4 mm scale and gill-cover relief: texture below the wall rule by design, not gated by it; whether it resolves depends on the unknown printer. The smooth file is the gated one.
- Closed, oriented, single-piece manifold, positive volume and box fit are checked on every file; global self-intersection is not checked.
- Engineered illustrative forms: not organisms and not a living or bio-printed construct.
- Masses are calculated from mesh volume at the Alpha Grey V1 card density (1220 kg/m3, cured); the prop resin is not chosen and nothing was weighed.

## Findings kept on record

- **relief_degenerate_triangles_before_cleanup** (fixed in these bytes, kept on record): The first v4 build (not committed) wrote scale-relief STLs that FAILED the validator: 3 triangles each under the 1e-10 mm2 degenerate cut (edges ~1e-5 mm, left by the 1e-5 mm grid snap), and the build did not gate relief validity. Fixes: the relief file is now gated on the validator, and every v4 export is simplified at 1e-4 mm after the snap and snapped again (volume change recorded per file). The v3 relief files were not re-exported; their smallest triangle is 1.07e-10 mm2, just above the cut.
- **glide_pose_prop_failed_dense_wall** (withheld from this set; unresolved): fish3d-v3c-daane-length-prop-glide-pose (v3b's assumed glide pose on v3c, membrane 1.2 x wall) was built and FAILED the 12,000-sample wall gate: minimum 0.975 mm against the 1.0 mm rule on its cambered fins (its 3,000-sample minimum was 1.066 mm, a pass). The camber is a vertical offset, so a membrane of thickness t measures t cos(slope) across its surface. It is not published. A thicker membrane (1.5 x wall) is proposed in V4_PENDING and will be built and checked as an additive revision. The frozen v3b prop uses the same camber with a 1.2 x membrane; its own 3,000-sample minimum is 1.079 mm.
