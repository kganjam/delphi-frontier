# What the system does

Companion to https://studio.morphoswarm.ai/#/about

## The problem

A paper describes a mechanism. Engineering asks what happens when that mechanism must work
alongside several others, under new constraints, in an organism nobody has built. Studying
one mechanism at a time hides the places where mechanisms disagree — and those
disagreements are where the missing science is.

So we picked a design goal demanding enough to force the collision: **engineer toward a
vertebrate glider reachable from a beloniform ancestor.** A fin must grow, bear load, move
through water and support a glide. Each requirement belongs to a different community, with
different units, assumptions and organisms.

## Two directions that must meet

- **Bottom up.** Fit known biology with equations and sourced parameters.
- **Top down.** Start from the goal — a launchable glider — and derive what tissue,
  material and flow properties it would require.

Where the two disagree, there is a question to work on. That is the frontier.

## The surfaces

### Studio — the design across eleven scales
molecular · macromolecular · subcellular · cellular · tissue · organ · neural · organism ·
developmental · collective · ecological.

Each panel holds its sources, models, equations, media **and its gap sentence**. A scale
with no model shows the gap, it is not filled in with something plausible. This is the
feature, not an omission: the gap list is the scientific output.

### Frontier — open questions as first-class records
Field questions from the literature, gaps the design exposed, and questions generated by
the work itself. Each carries its approach, the scales it touches and its state.

### Knowledge — claims that carry their conditions
A claim is one atomic assertion kept with its context (organism, stage, conditions), its
evidence, who or what asserted it, and its review status. It is a record that can be
disputed, not an established fact. Imported claims keep their upstream provenance and
licence; an upstream label is never local acceptance. Semantic search runs over 2,444
embedded documents with a graph view.

### Evidence — results you can replay
Sources with read-levels, preserved disagreements, replayable results, and artifacts with
sha256 digests. A media file is published only if its bytes match its declared digest.

### Engineering — bounded, checked calculation
A constraint solver over the design's own inequalities. It returns feasible, **unsat with
a conflict certificate**, or unknown. The certificate is then checked by Lean, and a
certificate with one number altered is rejected.

### System — the build inspecting itself
Specs, owner board, receipts, resources and unknowns, split into *author report* and
*measured*.

## The agent interface (MCP)

`https://studio.morphoswarm.ai/mcp`, Streamable HTTP, **21 tools**:

- **Read (18):** `frontier_search`, `frontier_get`, `frontier_neighborhood`,
  `frontier_events`, `frontier_artifacts`, `frontier_capabilities`, `frontier_discover`,
  `frontier_graph`, `frontier_catalogue`, `frontier_project_inspect`,
  `frontier_design_context`, `frontier_artifact_inspect`, `frontier_simulate`,
  `frontier_engineer`, `frontier_proofs`, `frontier_formalize`, `frontier_belief`,
  `frontier_plan_experiments`.
- **Write, opt-in (3):** `frontier_propose`, `frontier_lease`, `frontier_transition`.

Public reads need no login. Writes need an **individual contributor credential**, and each
request authenticates as its own principal — the server holds no shared token. A proposal
enters as `proposed` and waits for review elsewhere.

**Recorded interface check:** 12 of 12 checks passed on 2026-10-31, covering client
connectivity, search, schema discovery and **rejection of anonymous writes**, with
`scientific_records_written: 0`. Its scope line says plainly that this is connectivity,
not scientific acceptance.

## Where a person or an agent can intervene

At every one of the eleven scales: read what is recorded, comment, ask a question,
challenge a claim, or propose an edit. Drafts written in the browser stay in that browser
until a credentialed contribution is made; the page never holds a credential.
