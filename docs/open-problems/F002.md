## 2. Chosen system

**Zebrafish blastula BMP2b / Chordin**, Pomreinke et al. 2017 eLife 6:e25861 (PMC5658070, CC BY 4.0). Full text read as XML from Europe PMC (HTTP 200, 2026-11-01T16:44:46Z). Figure images and any source data were not read.

## 4. What a structure prediction can and cannot supply

- **Can:** a coordinate hypothesis with model-internal confidence; candidate interface residues for designing binding mutants. If the structure is confident and glycans and tags are modelled, a separate hydrodynamics calculation gives a hydrodynamic radius. That bounds D_free in buffer and could be checked against the FCS D_free above.
- **Cannot:** Kd, kon/koff, k1, D_eff in tissue, production rate, or gradient length.
- **Docking / complex / ΔΔG models:** these give a geometry or a relative score. Such a score becomes evidence about Kd only after calibration against measured Kd for the same family (SPR/BLI), with a pre-set error bound and a negative control that the model passes. The project's Fgfr2c control already failed that test.
- **Is a BioNeMo computation justified now? No.** It is not justified until (a) a matched measurement exists (fin-stage FRAP/FDAP/FCS for the same ligand, or a measured Kd for the same pair); (b) a pre-registered mapping from model output to a measurable quantity is written down (for example predicted R_h → D_free versus FCS, with the tolerance fixed in advance); and (c) the model passes a negative control.

## 5. Held-out calibration design and the consistency check

- **Model:** source–diffusion–clearance in 3D embryo geometry, with production in the clone and uniform D and k1.
- **Fixed inputs:** k1 (FDAP) and D_eff (FRAP).
- **Held-out observable:** the 60–75 min gradient profile (Fig. 3H+I), which was never used for the FRAP/FDAP fits.
- **Pre-set tolerance:** x50 within the reported band widened by a factor of 1.5.

`sdd_check.py` results. These are arithmetic on published values, not new findings.
- **Steady-state λ = √(D/k1):** 150 ± 15 µm for BMP2b-Dendra2 and 250 ± 15 µm for Chordin-Dendra2. It is **not applicable** here: k1·t = 0.32–0.35 at 60 min, so the measured gradients are far from steady state.
- **1D planar fixed-source surrogate:** x50 = 70–132 µm against the reported 30–60 µm. It **fails** the tolerance for every construct.
- **Spherical fixed-source surrogate:** pass or fail depends only on the *assumed* clone radius. BMP2b passes for R ≥ 35 µm and Chordin only for R ≥ 50 µm. A compact clone of 50–75 cells gives roughly R ≈ 28–42 µm, but that uses a cell diameter I did not verify.
- **BMP2b, gradient fit versus FRAP:** the authors' own 3D gradient fit gave D = 4 µm²/s. That is 2.0× the Dendra2 FRAP value (about 5 SE apart) and 1.5× the sfGFP value (about 2 SE apart). The gradient-fit value has no SE, so this is a tension, not a demonstrated inconsistency.

**Reading.** With D and k1 pinned, the held-out observable is controlled by quantities nobody measured: source geometry, source kinetics, fluorophore maturation and receptor saturation. A crude surrogate can therefore neither validate nor falsify the published values. The authors report that their own 3D model agrees (Fig. 3–figure supplement 2E–H); I did not re-run it. That agreement is the remaining uncertainty for this system. For the fin there is **documented insufficiency of evidence**: no matched transport measurement exists to hold out.

## 7. Blocked, and on whom

- **Matched fin measurements** (FRAP/FDAP/FCS of Shh, Fgf10 or BMP in the pectoral fin bud or fin fold, plus clone or source geometry): these need a wet lab with zebrafish transgenics and light-sheet FRAP. Nobody in this project has one.
- **A 3D re-run of the published BMP2b/Chordin model** with the per-embryo profiles: this needs the authors' source data or mesh (not found in the text I read; PyFRAP is the Müller lab's tool) or digitisation of Fig. 3H+I. It needs a transport/biophysics reviewer.
- **Binding constants** (BMP–Chordin, Fgf10–Fgfr2b Kd): these need SPR/BLI literature I have not searched, or a wet lab.
