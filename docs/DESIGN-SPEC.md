## THE VERDICT, FIRST

**This design does not fly, by two independent routes, and neither is close.**

- **Gliding** is short of the area needed to hold lift equal to weight by a factor that no published lift coefficient closes: it would need CL near 7 where the best steady value measured anywhere is about 1.75.

- **Powered flapping** needs a beat frequency of roughly 83 Hz. Fish white locomotory muscle produces NO power above about 12 Hz, and the superfast muscle that could reach it has never been sequenced in any animal, so the transgene the route depends on cannot be specified.

- **A latch-spring launch is also closed, and this one was a genuine candidate.** Two objections to it fell in turn -- the anatomical one (seahorses and pipefish are a characterised LaMSA system whose springs are tendons zebrafish also have) and the storage one (the tendon needed is 0.28-4.44% of body mass) -- and the 134% energy figure turned out to be a function of muscle fraction, with break-even at 53.9% against active teleosts reported above 60%. It closes on TRANSFER EFFICIENCY. Even a fish made entirely of muscle needs 54% of muscle work to arrive as body kinetic energy. The best efficiency measured for any fish fast-start is 39%, and a spring-loaded mechanical fish -- the directly analogous device, built and measured -- achieved 10%. A latch changes WHEN energy arrives, not how much survives the water.

- **Harvesting the wind is closed too, and for a structural reason.** Dynamic soaring supplies ENERGY, not LIFT, and every phase of a cycle presupposes an animal already flying. At 16% of its weight in lift this design cannot climb; its entire vertical range is the 7.8 cm its launch buys, against the metres of shear layer a soaring bird works in. A fish that cannot hold itself up cannot perform the manoeuvre that would give it the energy to hold itself up.

The sections below give the design anyway, scale by scale, because the parts that ARE achievable are specified in real genes with real magnitudes, and because a negative result is only useful if someone can see exactly where it fails.

## WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE ANSWER

Each of these is a specific measurement that does not exist, not a judgement call. They are listed because a negative result should name its own falsifiers.

- **The absolute power of superfast muscle, in W/kg, at ~83 Hz.** The route needs ~53 W/kg. Young & Rome 2001 measured the frequency ceilings but the paper is outside PMC's open-access subset, established by an efetch fetch returning 5,799 bytes with no body against 157,519 for a control.

- **A sequenced superfast swimbladder myosin.** Claim 889: every sonic-fish myosin in GenBank is a partial CARDIAC chain of 237-255 aa. This is wet-lab work; no model can act on a sequence that does not exist.

- **Whether a run-up and the escape increment add in quadrature or linearly.** Three rounds of searching found no study comparing escape speed from a moving start against from rest.

- **Whether two fin-overgrowth alleles stack.** Claim 598: the only epistasis experiment is a REVERSION, and its authors favour an additive model, which would RAISE the 2.65-fold ceiling. No combination has been published.

- **Fin elastic storage, measured directly.** Claim 592 answered the question by resonance instead, because nobody has measured the storage fraction.
