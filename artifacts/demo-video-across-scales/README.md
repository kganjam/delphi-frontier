The 60-second cut retains its earlier visuals and closing narration. It is a separate short edit, not a shortened copy of the final 90-second film.

This cut uses the approved third closing audition, with “you” emphasized visually and vocally. The scale overview and the original adversarial-design diagram each gained one second; the total remains 90 seconds. The TTS input spells the closing emphasis “YOO”; the script and captions retain “you”.

The film presents a biological-compiler vision using concept illustrations and recorded simulations with their limits. It does not establish a validated living organism or functional whole-organism DNA. Full media decode and audio checks were run on the exact video bytes; the scientific simulations and proofs were not rerun for this film.
