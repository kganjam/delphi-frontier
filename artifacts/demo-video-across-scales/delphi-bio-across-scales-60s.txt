Type: “Design a fish that can fly.”

That’s the promise of a biological compiler.

Delphi Bio and MorphoSwarm Studio bring a decentralized swarm of agents to the challenge—proposing, simulating, and challenging each other’s designs. They apply mathematical biology.

Can oxygen delivery support launch power? Could proteins make fins tougher without adding weight? Which signals shape growth? How would a school coordinate—and change its ecosystem?

Bigger fins may help lift. What do they cost in drag, strength, and energy?

We extract mathematical laws from literature and data, make assumptions explicit, and formally check deductions. Compatible models become building blocks, with control knobs to explore.

Agents propose simulations and experiments across scales. Each simulation should answer a design question. What we learn returns to Delphi—with evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, and limits—so the next design starts smarter.

The pieces of verified biology are coming together.

The destination: candidate DNA, with a plan to build and test it.
