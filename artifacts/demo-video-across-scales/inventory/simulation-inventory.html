Proposed questions across all 11 scales

molecular. Which molecular structures and interactions could support the intended material or signaling function?

macromolecular. How would protein assemblies transfer forces while keeping a fin light and tough?

subcellular. Can respiration and ATP supply meet the intended demand within oxygen and nutrient limits?

cellular. Which signals set cell fates, and when should responses stay local or spread?

tissue. How can growth, repair and material organization produce flexible, damage-tolerant tissue?

organ. Can fins carry load and change shape while other organs deliver enough oxygen?

neural. Which sensing, steering and reflex loops can recover from disturbances?

organism. How should launch, lift, drag, energy use and return to water work together?

developmental. Where, when and how much should each structure grow, and when should growth stop?

collective. Can local cues coordinate a group without collisions or unstable cascades?

ecological. What would change for predators, prey and food webs, and which fitness tradeoffs could emerge?
