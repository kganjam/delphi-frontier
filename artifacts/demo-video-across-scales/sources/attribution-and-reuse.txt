SOURCE ATTRIBUTION AND REUSE STATUS

Original Delphi deck compositions, animated sphere and embedded illustrations
Source: https://delphibio.org/deck/
The production used the published November 3, 2026 deck. Native artwork is retained without enhancement or regeneration; the capture and native-art manifests record source identity and hashes. The fish illustration and cell/drug-discovery illustration also served as style references for the three new concept images.

MorphoSwarm Studio and Delphi computational media
Sources: https://studio.morphoswarm.ai/ and https://delphibio.org/
Underlying artifact repository: https://github.com/kganjam/delphi-frontier
Individual original titles, model attributions, artifact URLs and manifest links are retained in the inventory and source manifests. Some clips reproduce published models or reuse specimen data. Those upstream model/data attributions must remain attached when the footage is reused.

New concept illustrations
Energy supply, fin material and ecology were generated using the built-in image-generation tool from the exact archived prompts and original Delphi style-reference images. The archive retains the resulting image bytes and SHA-256 values. The prompts support provenance; rerunning a generative tool is not expected to reproduce identical image bytes. These images illustrate design questions, not measured outcomes.

Narration
Provider/model: MiniMax speech-2.8-hd.
Voice: English_expressive_narrator.
The archived recordings, synthesis settings, scripts and delivery timings are the inputs for rebuilding the films. A fresh synthesis request is optional and can change timing or delivery. Credentials and private service configuration are excluded.

Licensing status
This production audit did not establish a separate redistribution license for every upstream illustration, model, dataset, voice asset or font. Public availability is recorded as provenance, not as a grant of rights. No new blanket license or independent rights clearance is asserted by this note. Preserve existing source notices and attribution; any repository-level license should distinguish authored production code/copy from upstream media and data whose terms remain with their sources. System font files are not included as freely redistributable assets.
