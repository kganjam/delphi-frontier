Scientific distinctions retained in the films
- Collagen-fragment molecular dynamics illustrates short molecular motion. It does not measure respiration or establish a lightweight fin material.
- ERK, electrical-cell and BETSE models illustrate recorded signaling or perturbation scenarios. Transfer to a specific fish tissue remains untested.
- The early fin-flexibility shot uses the declared linear segment of a beam surrogate. It is not calibrated tissue mechanics. Static aeroelastic iterations assume stiffness.
- The CFD reference check failed and fin thickness remains unresolved. A visible wake is not evidence of validated hydrodynamics.
- Schooling and glide movies use encoded rules and declared model assumptions. Ecosystem consequences remain proposed design questions.
- Six turntables show candidate print geometry, not observed organisms or tested flight. The two comparison sets use different display scales.
- The four composable interfaces pass mass, area and span into glide, and span into a load bridge. They establish compatible shared inputs, not validated links from respiration to flight or calibrated cross-scale biology.
- The experiment animation explains a recorded prospective comparison: an independent load reading plus deflection versus two deflection readings, under assumed noise. It shows no experimental outcome.
- The allometry visual is a recorded refit using additional reused specimen rows. More data worsened held-out transfer in that example; the film does not claim guaranteed improvement.
- Formal deductions apply under stated assumptions. Model fidelity and proof checking do not establish biological validity.
