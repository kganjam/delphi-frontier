Cantilever guard: a small-deflection bound with satisfiable hypotheses

recorded compile matches these files Lean (version 4.35.0 · Mathlib 56a005855e

For every positive P, L, E and I, if the end-load law 3EId = PL^3 holds and the bending stiffness satisfies 10 P L^2 <= 3 E I, then the deflection satisfies d <= L/10. The hypotheses are shown to be satisfiable, and the stiffness hypothesis is shown to be necessary.

IN PLAIN TERMS
A fin ray bending under a small load. The theorem gives a bound, and a second theorem shows that its conditions can actually be met, so the bound is not true only because nothing qualifies.

The slab is a derived 1-D analogue, not a Grimes equation; neither geometry proves repair, barrier recovery, actuation, motion or organism function.
