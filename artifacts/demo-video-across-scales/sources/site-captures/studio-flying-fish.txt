Fin-beam load bridge is scenario-level

aerodynamics organ source-only prompt

The organ panel calls the fin-beam model a scoped elastic surrogate. docs/model-coupling.md marks the load-to-beam bridge scenario-level: mean lift-pressure proxy times a declared tributary width, a declared ray-length fraction and an assumed EI. There is no calibrated fin stiffness or measured ray pressure.

Challenge to write: Which loading regime and measured stiffness would falsify this bridge?

Glide release convention drives much of the range

aerodynamics organism source-only prompt

The glide report uses a release at 1.5 times stall speed from 1 m, equivalent to release heights from about 1.8 m to 32.6 m. It says this convention drives much of the range and is a model artifact.

Challenge to write: Compare a sourced fixed-speed or fixed-height release; what observable would reject either?
