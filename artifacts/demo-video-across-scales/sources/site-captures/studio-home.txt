What the body and fins are sized from (N1 baseline reference)

Candidate N1: standard length 158.8 mm, wing span 211.7 mm, wing area 5699 mm², mass 0.05 kg.

Measured here: pectoral fin length against standard length in 49 preserved flying fish specimens (Cheilopogon furcatus, Exocoetus volitans, Hirundichthys rondeleti; preserved specimens, Harvard Museum of Comparative Zoology) follows length ∝ SL1.058, 95% bootstrap interval 1.021 to 1.099, R² 0.983. Species and sizes are pooled. Source: Daane et al. 2021, Data S1A, fit made in this repository.
Published, raw data unavailable: Fish 1990 reports the pectoral fins as Cypselurus (76 ± 0.6%) to Exocoetus (91.8 ± 0.4%) of wing area by genus, and 63.1% ± 0.5 of caudal area in the lower lobe (pooled). UNAVAILABLE: 199 specimens were measured; the paper publishes only the fits and ranges below.
