How much do the assumed parameters matter?

assumed engineering scenarios same-model numeric checks, not biological confidence How do assumed mass, local reaction delay and hinge stiffness alter the paired effect of neighbor cues on actual airborne motion and bounded actuator work? Seven assumed settings (seven-point axial sensitivity design; no interaction estimates) were each run for two seeds, with and without the neighbour cue: 28 scenarios, 28 computed, 28 passing the half-step numeric gate. All 28 stopped at the edge of the reused VLM domain (effective fin alpha outside reused VLM domain), so every endpoint is a domain stop, not a landing or escape outcome.
