Manifest format

Fields the build reads: title, scales[], evidence_kind, model{name,context}, time{units,note}, video{file,sha256,note}, poster, numerical_checks[], parameter_unknowns[], open_questions[], couplings[{from,to,status}]. The video file must sit beside the manifest. The runtime serves .mp4 and .webm under site/artifacts/ with range requests; the build copies a video there only when its bytes match the declared sha256.
