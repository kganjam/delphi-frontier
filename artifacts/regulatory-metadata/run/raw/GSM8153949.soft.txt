!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = For fin amputation, we anesthetized one-year old fish with 0.16g/L MS-222 in system water, and then removed the distal half of their caudal fin with sterilized razor blades. The fish were then sent back to circulating water system for recovery. We collected regenerating fin tissue from fish by doing secondary fin amputation at the primary cutting plane, with the same anesthesia and recovery procedures. For 0 dpa sample, we collected two segments of fin rays from each fish after removing the distal half of their caudal fin. Each time point has two biological replicates. Tissues were collected in 1mL 1 X PBS on ice. Fresh tissues were treated with 1mL 1X TrypLE Express enzyme (Gibco) expression in 30.5 ˚C with rotation for around 30 minutes. Isolation status was checked under microscope every 15 minutes after gentle mixing. Once the cells were well-dissociated, cells were centrifuged at 500 × g for 3 min at 4 °C to remove supernatants. 1 mL of 1X cold PBS buffer was used to wash cells, followed by centrifuging at 500 × gfor 3 min at 4 °C. For 0 dpa sample, supernatants were discarded, and the remaining fin tissue were further dissociated into a single-cell suspension with 750 µl of 0.5 mg/mL Liberase DL (Roche) in enzyme-free Cell Dissociation Buffer (Gibco) at 30.5 °C for 15 min with gentle agitation. Liberase DL contains highly purified Collagenase I and Collagenase II and facilitates cell dissociation from the intact fin tissue. After Liberase dissociation, samples were centrifuged at 500 x g for 3 min at 4 °C and supernatants were discarded, then washed with 1mL pre-cold 1X PBS and supernatants were discarded again. After enzyme dissociation, cells were filtered through 30µm or 40µm strainer followed by centrifugation at 500 × g for 3 min at 4˚C and washed in 500µl sort buffer for one time. Then, cells were resuspended in 300 µl DNase solution with pipetting mix 5 times and incubated on ice for 5 min. After adding 1mL 1X cold PBS with 0.04% BSA, samples were then centrifuged at 500 × g for 3 min at 4 °C. This wash step was repeated for two more times. Then cells were resuspended in 0.1X Lysis buffer with gentle pipetting, then incubated on ice for 3 min. 1 mL Wash buffer were added and tubes were gently inverted 3-4 times. Then, samples were centrifuged 300 × g for 3 min at 4˚C and supernatant were removed. This wash step was repeated 2 more times followed by checking nuclei status under microscope. After last round centrifugation, 10 µl supernatant were left in the tube, then 50µl of 1X Diluted nuclei buffer was added followed by centrifugation at 300xg for 3 min at 4˚C. After removing supernatants, samples were resuspended with 20 µL of 1x diluted nuclei buffer. Trypan blue was used to count nuclei. The final nuclei concentration was 2,000-2,500 nuclei/µL. The chromium Next GEM Single Cell Multiome ATAC + Gene Expression Reagent Bundle from 10x Genomics was used for library preparation. Sequencing was performed using Novaseq 6000 (Illumina).
