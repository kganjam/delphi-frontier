R1 VLM convergence: thin-surface vortex-lattice (horseshoes, bound at panel 1/4 chord, collocation 3/4 chord, straight
trailing legs along +x). Found that the committed lattice placed collocation points using the polygon chord at
mid-span while the bound vortex used the panel-edge chords; on curved outlines collocation points fell outside their own
panels (up to 2 chords upstream at 24 chordwise panels), so CL drifted with chordwise refinement for all variants
(e.g. 0.250 -> 0.217 at fixed span count). Fix: collocation at the bilinear 3/4-chord point of the panel's own corners;
then CL is independent of chordwise count to 1e-4 and changes ~1% for 24->48 spanwise. The "v3b" variant has a
non-planar (cambered/twisted) mid-surface; its normals are taken from the source surface (bilinear quads) on a planar
lattice. Corrected v3b CL(4 deg)=0.16, CL(0)=-0.066 vs flat 0.23: the pose surface slopes upward toward the trailing
edge (mean normal x-component -0.095), i.e. ~5 deg negative effective incidence.
