Could a biological design be checked as carefully as a program? MorphoSwarm Frontier is an early step toward Lean for biology: shared claims, equations, assumptions and evidence that people and agents can inspect.

We start with a demanding question: could a zebrafish acquire the structures needed to glide? Published fin-growth genetics supplies hypotheses, not a demonstrated route to flight.

For a fin modeled as an elastic beam, we formalized a stiffness condition that bounds deflection. Lean compiled the theorem and rejected a corrupted law.

That checks a mathematical implication; whether the model describes a living fin still requires evidence. The studio connects bounded results across scales and exposes missing links as open questions.

Through MCP, contributors can retrieve claims, submit work and contest evidence. Our goal is reusable, checkable biology: proof establishes what follows from a model; experiments establish when it applies.
