# Errata to docs/epithelial-repair-card.md (edc1e10), from independent review

The card shipped here is owner 09's corrected `46eb7ff`, byte-for-byte; 09 has fixed items 1 to 3 below in the card itself (gate tally, 625 filled versus 1875 residual, and the 1.414 bracket wording), so they are listed only to show what was corrected. Items 4 to 8 are rendering and caption points that the card does not carry, and apply to how the site shows it. Use this note and the manifest, which count from `artifacts/epithelial/results-r2.json`.

1. **Gate counts.** The card and handoff say nine of thirteen gates pass. The results file has 8 pass (G1, G1b, G2, G3, G5, G7, G9, G11), 3 fail (G4, G10, G12), 1 descriptive (G6: three seeds of one model, no bound assigned) and 1 unevaluable (G8). G6 is not a pass.
2. **Geometric floor arithmetic (card section 4).** The text takes 1875 nodes as filled to give a 25% floor. 1875 is the residual gap, 40000 - 61 x 625. With 61 surviving cells, 625 nodes are filled (25% of the 2500-node gap). The measured passive closure, 22.0% (arm F1-no-drive-NEGATIVE), is unaffected and stays beside every closure figure. No 25% figure is shown in the site cards until 09 corrects the card's labels.
3. **G9 and the 1.414 value.** 1.414 is the geometric mean of the observed bracket (1, 2) at k=4, not an observed point threshold. One doubling of interface energy supports one conditional out-of-sample bracket check in this model; it does not establish J scaling at k=7 or exact proportionality, and the phrase "doubling must double the threshold" is a tested model hypothesis, not a physical law. Gap-size scaling is refuted in this model (G12 fail; G8 unevaluable).
4. **Unchanged.** Model time and sweeps, not minutes; no tissue or biological validation; no purse-string closure is modelled.
5. **Cell counts.** 625 nodes is the nominal target volume and scale anchor, not an observed mean. The unwounded arm starts with 65 cells (logger and first TIFF), a mean of 40000/65 = 615.4 nodes; A1 and F1 have 61 after 4 are removed. Frozen runs and scale are not changed.
6. **Passive floor.** 22.04% (F1) is observed in that context only, not a universal causal amount to subtract; show it beside a matched baseline. The ideal 25% relaxation arithmetic is consistent with it, but volume, perimeter and Metropolis dynamics remain and no isolated mechanism contribution was measured.
7. **G1 and G1b.** G1 is an identity from the gap definition. G1b compares logger and TIFF only at the 21 shared times of 41 TIFF frames per arm; "every frame agrees" overstates it. It is a readout-consistency check on one engine state.
8. **Horizon and gap size.** CLOSED and ARRESTED are operational labels at a finite 1000 model-unit horizon. The k=4 and k=7 bracket intervals overlap, so their differing midpoints alone do not refute a constant Phi; k=1 never arrests, G8 is unevaluable and G12 failed: counter-evidence, not rescued.
