Not checked

  • Global self-intersection, slicing, supports, cups and minima. These depend on printer, resin and orientation, and PreForm checks them.
  • No physical print and no PreForm run. Resin volume and print time come only from the slicer.
  • Aerodynamics of the prop or the design. Visual likeness is not aerodynamic fidelity, and the glide simulation has not run on these candidates.
  • Biological feasibility, ancestry and evolutionary reachability. None are claimed.
