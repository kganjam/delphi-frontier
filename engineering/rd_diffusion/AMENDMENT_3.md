* **Consequence for this pilot's `mode_tv` / dominant-mode metric**: it has no amplitude gate. For near-uniform (non-patterning) solver fields the dominant mode is numerically arbitrary, so conditions at non-patterning settings contribute noise to the mode statistics for the solver, the DDPM and the baselines alike. The frozen primary mode results are reported as frozen, with this caveat attached; they are not evidence about pattern selection unless the solver amplitude supports it.

## Registered secondary stratification (declared before results)
`secondary_eval.py` additionally reports medians of mode_tv, the interpolated W1 and the exact W1 separately for conditions whose **solver median std(u) is at least 1e-3 model units ("patterned")** and below it ("near_uniform"), with condition counts. 1e-3 is chosen well above the 1e-12 amplitude at which the numerical audit saw mode flips and well below the initial perturbation scale (0.05 x u*); it is a declared reporting gate, not an estimate.
