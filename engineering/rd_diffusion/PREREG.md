# Preregistration: conditional DDPM surrogate for a 1-D Schnakenberg reaction-diffusion field

Written and committed **before any training or held-out evaluation**. Single-reader work (Claude Sonnet 5.5, market worker), not independently reviewed. Everything is SIMULATED model-unit data from a forward solver. This is an implementation pilot of an existing method class (conditional diffusion surrogates; cf. Bastek et al., arXiv:2403.14404; Comlekoglu et al., PMC12570967); it claims no new diffusion methodology, no fish or fin biology, and no calibration to any measurement.

## Data (generated inside the job by the frozen solver; no fabricated or digitised fields)
Batched torch float64 port of the solver (Strang splitting, exact DCT-II diffusion, RK4 reaction, dt 0.02 tau, N = 64, K = 20 initial modes, perturbation amplitude eps = 0.05 as an explicit assumption), equivalence to the numpy solver tested to 1e-9. Parameters (a, b, Dv, L) drawn uniformly from the prior in `config.json` with a fixed table seed; they are assumptions, not estimates. Fields saved at tau = 50, 100, 150, 200. Runs that are non-finite or exceed |u|,|v| > 1000 are flagged `failed`, excluded from training and counted in the receipts (negatives are kept in the receipts, not hidden).
Splits are by whole setting or whole initial-condition seed: **train** settings 0-1399 x seeds 0,1,2; **test_param** settings 1450-1749 (never trained on) x seeds 0,1; **test_seed** settings 0-299 x seed 3 (training settings, unseen initial conditions); **ensemble** settings 1450-1489 x 32 seeds 100-131 (for conditional distributions); **dev** 1400-1449 only for pipeline debugging and the smoke control, never reported. No (setting, seed) pair appears in two splits (tested). Snapshots of one run share a split. Independent cardinality: 1400 training settings x 3 seeds (4200 runs, 16,800 rows, rows within a run are correlated). Normalisation (per-channel mean/std) is fitted on training rows only.

## Model and training (frozen; no tuning on holdouts)
1-D U-Net (1.35 M parameters, replicate padding = zero-flux ends, GroupNorm, SiLU) on a 2 x 64 field; conditioning vector (a, b, Dv, L, tau) scaled to [-1,1] by the prior range, added to the diffusion-step embedding; epsilon-prediction DDPM, T = 200, cosine schedule; AdamW lr 3e-4 to 3e-5 cosine, weight decay 1e-4, batch 256, 30,000 steps, grad clip 1, EMA 0.999 (EMA weights are the model evaluated), seed 0. No physics loss and no guidance; conditioning does not guarantee the equations hold, so the evaluation measures consistency. The checkpoint is saved and hashed before any held-out data is generated.

## Evaluation (all computed after the checkpoint is frozen; thresholds below are reporting criteria, not statistics)
1. **Conditional ensembles** (160 conditions = 40 held-out settings x 4 times): 32 generated samples vs the 32-seed solver ensemble per condition. Metrics per condition: dominant-mode distribution total variation (mode of u from the DCT-II, index 0 excluded), whether the most frequent mode agrees, amplitude (std of u over x) 1-D Wasserstein distance and mean error each relative to the solver mean amplitude, fraction non-finite, fraction with negative u or v.
2. **Baselines at the same inputs**: nearest training setting (pool of its 3 seeds, resampled to 32), nearest-5-settings pool, deterministic mean field of the nearest 10 settings (the conditional-mean baseline), and a **null**: the same DDPM with conditions shuffled across settings (same tau). Nearest = L1 distance in the prior-scaled (a, b, Dv, L).
3. **Held-out marginals**: one sample per held-out condition for test_param and test_seed; per-tau amplitude W1 and mode-distribution TV against the direct solver, plus negativity/non-finite fractions.
4. **Short rollout consistency** (not a steady-state residual, because snapshots are not steady): relative change of u over a 10-tau solver continuation from generated vs from solver states, reported as medians.
5. **Timing**: 1024 generated fields, 2 warm-up and 3 timed batches with device synchronisation; solver time on the same device and CPU (64 runs). Training and data-generation cost are reported separately; no speedup claim is made from these numbers alone.
Reporting: medians over conditions and paired win counts (DDPM lower/tie/higher) per baseline; no significance statistics and no confidence intervals are invented. Descriptive success statement for the pilot: "the checkpoint was actually trained and evaluated"; a result in which the DDPM is worse than a baseline or the shuffled-condition null is reported as such.

## Failure handling and what will not be claimed
Missing holdouts or a truncated output are a failed run, not an empty valid result. No tuning of any value above after seeing held-out metrics: a second run would be a new, separately registered run. Not claimed: biological validity, grown scales, a fin or fish model, calibrated units, equation satisfaction by the surrogate, generality beyond the 1-D Schnakenberg prior box, or any speed advantage.
