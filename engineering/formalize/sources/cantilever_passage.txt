Euler–Bernoulli beam theory is limited to small deflections of a [[beam (structure)|beam]] that is subjected to lateral loads only, causing [[Elasticity (physics)|elastic]] bending.

[Alben, Madden & Lauder 2007, PMC2359861, Section 3 text]
In our experiment, we have found using three-point bending tests that the ‘composite’ Young's modulus corresponding to the whole structure is E ≈10 8 –10 9 N m −2 , with the average area moment of inertia I ≈10 −6 cm 4 . Hence, the measured B = EI ≈10 −6 –10 −5 J m.
