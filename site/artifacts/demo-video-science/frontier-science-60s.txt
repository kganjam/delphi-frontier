Lean for biology: make scientific claims precise enough for people and machines to check.

That foundation could let agents reuse knowledge, combine models and solve design constraints across scales.

We compiled a fin-beam stiffness theorem in Lean and rejected a corrupted law.

Now use a flying-fish design to connect genes, growth, mechanics and behavior. Each missing connection becomes a testable question.

Frontier brings those claims, simulations and evidence together through an open MCP interface. The ambition is cumulative biological engineering: propose, check, test and improve, with every contribution available to the next investigator.
