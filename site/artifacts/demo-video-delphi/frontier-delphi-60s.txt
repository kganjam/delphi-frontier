Delphi is building toward Lean for biology: a shared foundation of biological mathematics that people and machines can inspect.

Formalize the claim, its assumptions and its evidence. Then reuse that knowledge to solve constraints and explore new designs.

In Frontier, Lean compiled a fin-beam theorem and rejected a corrupted law.

A flying-fish design shows the ambition: connect genes, growth, mechanics and behavior into a biological compiler.

Its intended output is candidate DNA, a build plan and validation tests. Through an open MCP interface, contributors can share results, challenge claims and advance the next question together.
